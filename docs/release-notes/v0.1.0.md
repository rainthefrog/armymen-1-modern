## Army Men Modern v0.1.0 (Alpha)

> **Early Alpha Build:** This is an early public alpha (`v0.1.0`) for testing and feedback. Expect occasional rough edges and breaking changes to quicksaves between updates.

A drop-in modernization mod (`ddraw.dll`) for the original **Army Men (1998)** PC game. It preserves the full campaign, missions, and visual identity while making the game play smoothly on modern Windows displays, high-refresh monitors, and Steam Deck / Linux (Proton / Wine).

**Bring your own game.** This download contains no Army Men assets, executables, HD packs, or Army Men II audio files. It patches the unmodified GOG release of `Armymen.exe` in memory when the game starts, and uses the original artwork by default.

### What's in the mod

#### Display, widescreen & rendering
- **True widescreen and ultrawide gameplay:** extends the visible battlefield instead of stretching 640x480, with presets from 720p through 4K plus custom resolutions, resizable windows, and borderless fullscreen (`F11` / `Alt+Enter`).
- **Direct3D 11 presentation & high-refresh pacing:** replaces the legacy exclusive 8-bit DirectDraw path with modern GPU rendering, crisp/smoothed scaling options, proper DPI awareness, and accurate game speed at 60, 120, 144 Hz and above.
- **Visual readability & polish:** sharper menu and radio text rendered at output resolution, optional battlefield zoom (`Ctrl+Wheel` / `Ctrl+PgUp`/`PgDn`), optional CRT filter (`F9`), map-edge fog, fitted videos, see-through cover for Sarge, and fixed layering for building fronts, damaged roofs, helicopters, and airborne objects.

#### Modern controls & keybindings
- **Army Men II-style mouse + WASD controls:** left-click ground to walk with A* pathfinding (double-click to sprint, hold to steer), left-click enemies to shoot, left-click empty vehicles to board, right-click or `Shift+Left` to force-fire, and WASD movement in screen directions with smooth camera follow and configurable camera lead.
- **Modern stance & throw aiming:** click, double-click, or press-and-drag on Sarge (or open the radial menu over him) for Stand / Kneel / Prone / Dive; grenade and mortar aiming keeps the target under the pointer with a blast-radius preview and long-range camera peek.
- **Configurable bindings:** customize keyboard and extra mouse-button (`Middle`, `Mouse4`, `Mouse5`) tap/hold/double gestures inside **Mod Settings** or the `Shift+F10` panel, with automatic conflict swapping and reset-to-defaults.

#### HUD, minimap & tactical guidance
- **Modular scalable HUD:** separated and independently scalable message, weapon, health, squad, air-support, and minimap groups (or turn off **Modern HUD layout** to keep the original centered 1x HUD).
- **Upgraded minimap & pinned objectives:** higher-contrast terrain, outlined army-colored blips, highlighted Sarge marker, detailed hover tooltips (army, unit/vehicle type, soldiers left, health, distance), pinned objective text, blinking target sectors, and an optional world-space **mission route** guiding Sarge through objectives.
- **Command radial:** hold Middle Mouse (or `B`) to issue squad orders and call recon, paratroopers, or air strikes without losing the original hotkeys; Attack and Defend support precise target-picking on both the battlefield and minimap.

#### Weapons, equipment & squad
- **Six weapon positions & stacking:** carry up to six weapons (A1–B3) with mouse-wheel cycling, quick-swap (`Q`), duplicate-pickup ammo stacking up to 99, and weapon/charge carry-over across multi-part scenarios (and Play Again).
- **Enemy weapon drops & medkits:** non-rifle enemy specialists drop their weapon as a real pickup crate on death; medkits walked over at full health are carried (up to 3) and can heal Sarge or wounded squadmates (`H`).
- **Reworked mines & minesweeper:** click-to-place mines with range ring and tile validation; passive minesweeper detection with visual/audio proximity cues, safe revealed mines, and click-to-walk-and-collect disarming.
- **Squad improvements:** tougher, regenerating friendly soldiers with wounded health bars, squad sprinting to keep up with Sarge, and Alpine medical tents healing your squad as well as Sarge.

#### Combat, vehicles & quicksaves
- **Smarter, fairer combat:** selectable height rules (**Modern**, **AM2**, or **Original**), stance damage and low-cover rules, enemy awareness indicators (could-see / noticing / targeting / lost), enemy class hover labels, Sarge health regeneration, and extended direct-fire range matched to the wider view.
- **Vehicles:** click-to-drive pathfinding with reversing and multi-point turns, smooth independent turret aiming, overlap unsticking, **enemy vehicle capture** (drive empty enemy vehicles), and **vehicle bail-out** (Sarge survives vehicle destruction with minor damage and brief invulnerability).
- **Persistent quicksaves:** three save slots (`Insert` save, `Home` load, `End` cycle slot) stored as files in `SaveGames\` that survive closing the game and can be loaded directly from the campaign screen.
- **Classic preset & optional packs:** toggle **Classic gameplay** for original mechanics with the modern renderer, or supply separate optional folders for HD art (`hd\`), Army Men II sounds (`am2_sounds\`), and Army Men II music (`am2_music\`).
- **Built-in updater:** checks GitHub Releases on startup and offers to download, verify (`ddraw.dll.sha256`), swap `ddraw.dll` in place, and restart automatically (can be triggered manually or disabled in **Gameplay > Check for Updates...** / `[update] check=0`).
- **Stability fixes:** extensive fixes for crashes on stale objects/vehicle IDs/weapons, mission restarts, Play Again, later scenario parts, cursor alignment, and Alt+Tab/window focus.

### Install on Windows (GOG)
1. Download `ddraw.dll` from this release.
2. Copy `ddraw.dll` into your GOG Army Men (1998) game folder, right beside `Armymen.exe`.
3. Launch the game normally.

No INI file, manifest, installer, or HD-art folder is required—the mod uses sensible built-in defaults and writes `armymen-mod.ini` automatically when you change options in **Mod Settings**. Your original `Armymen.exe` is never modified.

### Install on Linux / Steam Deck / Bazzite (Proton or Wine)
1. Download `ddraw.dll` and place it beside `Armymen.exe` in your Army Men installation folder.
2. In Steam (Properties > General > Launch Options) or your Lutris / Heroic runner environment, set:
   ```text
   WINEDLLOVERRIDES="ddraw=n,b" %command%
   ```
3. Launch the game normally.

### Updating & uninstalling
- **Automatic update:** when a newer alpha or full release is posted on GitHub, the mod prompts at startup to download, verify its SHA-256 checksum, replace `ddraw.dll`, and restart. You can also check manually via **Gameplay > Check for Updates...** (windowed mode) or disable startup checks with `[update] check=0` in `armymen-mod.ini`.
- **Manual update:** replace `ddraw.dll` with the newer version. Your `armymen-mod.ini` settings and campaign saves stay intact.
- **Uninstall:** delete `ddraw.dll` (and optionally `armymen-mod.ini`, `armymen-mod.log`, and `SaveGames\`). The original game returns untouched.

### Useful shortcuts
- **`F10`** — Open **Mod Settings** (`Shift+F10` for the compact overlay panel & keybinding editor)
- **`F11`** / **`Alt+Enter`** — Toggle borderless fullscreen
- **`F9`** — Toggle CRT filter
- **`Insert` / `Home` / `End`** — Quicksave / Quickload / Cycle save slot
- **`Ctrl + Wheel`** or **`Ctrl + PgUp / PgDn`** — Battlefield zoom
- **`Pause`** / **`Ctrl + -`** / **`Ctrl + =`** / **`Ctrl + 0`** — Pause or adjust game speed

### Tester reports & diagnostics
- Enable **Logging** in Mod Settings (or `[debug] log=1` in `armymen-mod.ini`), reproduce the issue, and attach `armymen-mod.log` (the previous run is kept as `armymen-mod.prev.log`).
- Optional session recording is off by default in release builds; testers can enable it in `armymen-mod.ini` with `[debug] record_runs=1` (writes `.jsonl` traces to `recordings\`; press `Ctrl+Shift+B` in game to drop a checkpoint bookmark).

### Compatibility notes
- Built for the **GOG Windows release** of Army Men (1998).
- Quicksaves are tied to the game/mod build and display dimensions; incompatible saves are refused safely without corrupting the mission.
- Optional HD art and Army Men II audio packs are separate and not included in this release.
